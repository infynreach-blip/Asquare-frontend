What Is a Turnkey Workplace Technology Integrator?

A Turnkey workplace technology integrator combines different technology needs into one plan.

Rather than handling each technology separately, companies can think of their workplace setup as a complete system. This can involve structured cabling, network connectivity, meeting-room technology, AV systems, and other ICT requirements.

Our role is to understand what the workplace needs, bring the technology parts together, and create a setup that helps the company work the way it really does.

This approach can be very helpful for companies moving into an office or doing a complete workplace upgrade, where technology needs to be aligned with the physical design of the space.

Smart Office Interior Technology Design

A smart workplace is not simply an office filled with technology. The technology should have a purpose and fit naturally into how people use the space in a workplace.

Our smart office interior technology design approach looks at how employees, teams, visitors, and management interact with the workplace, always keeping the broader work environment in mind.

Technology can be planned around areas such as:

Workstations

Work areas need reliable network and connectivity points. These support computers, communication systems, and other business devices.

Meeting Rooms

Meeting spaces may require displays, cameras, microphones, speakers, conferencing equipment, and reliable connectivity for communication.

Boardrooms

Executive meeting spaces require an integrated audiovisual system that supports professional presentation and communication capabilities.

Common Areas

Technology requirements in reception areas, collaboration spaces, breakout areas, and other shared spaces can be considered as part of the overall workplace infrastructure.

IT and Equipment Areas

Network equipment and related infrastructure must be placed carefully, connected properly, and kept easily accessible for ongoing management. The goal is to build technology infrastructure that supports the workplace without interfering with its design or usability.

ICT Solutions for Office Renovations and Fit-Outs

Many existing offices need technology upgrades when businesses grow, update their workspace, or change how their teams work.

An office renovation might require new network points, additional cabling, upgrading meeting-room technologies, improving audio-visual systems, or changing current infrastructure.

Before starting any work, it’s helpful to review the existing technology setup. This shows what can be retained, upgraded, moved, or replaced.

This review supports decisions and keeps interruptions to the workplace to a minimum.
