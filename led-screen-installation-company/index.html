Understanding Technical LED Screen Details Before Installation

Selecting and installing the right display system requires evaluating key technical parameters. Our team guides you through every detail during the planning phase:

Technical Parameter Engineering Consideration Installation Impact
Pixel Pitch (mm) Distance from the center of one LED to the center of the next (e.g., P0.9 to P3.9) Determines minimum viewing distance and display resolution. Fine pitch requires higher mounting precision.
Cabinet Construction Die-cast aluminum vs. sheet metal cabinets Dictates structural weight loads, heat dissipation requirements, and frame alignment tolerances.
Access Architecture Front-service (magnetic module extraction) vs. Rear-service access Drives recess depth, mounting bracket selection, and ongoing service accessibility.
Power & Thermal Load Peak/Average power consumption (Watts/m²) and BTU heat output Requires calculated dedicated circuits, rack ventilation, and thermal management.
Controller Protocols Synchronous vs. Asynchronous sending cards and video processors Configures network infrastructure, input source mapping, and multi-window processing.
