What Are Structured Cabling Systems?

Structured cabling systems serve as the backbone of a building's communication setup, providing an organized framework that supports various networking technologies. Rather than relying on unstructured cabling, structured cabling follows a standardized workflow by mapping cable routes, connection points, and equipment locations to create a unified network infrastructure.

These systems can connect a wide range of devices, including:

Computers and workstations
Network switches
Routers
Servers
Network access points
Voice communication systems
Security systems

When executed properly, a structured cabling system not only enhances connectivity but also simplifies network maintenance, troubleshooting, and future upgrades.
